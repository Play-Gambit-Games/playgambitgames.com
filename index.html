Reading the Odds

RTP, or Return to Player, is the theoretical percentage of every dollar wagered that a game pays back, calculated across millions of simulated spins. A 96% RTP means the game is designed to return 96 cents for every dollar staked, over the very long run.

96% is not what happens in any one session. That number is a long-run theoretical average, not a per-session guarantee. A hundred spins tonight can land far above it or far below it. Both are normal. The average only becomes visible at scale, the way a coin only settles near 50/50 after thousands of flips, not ten.
