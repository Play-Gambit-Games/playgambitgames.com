Reading the odds

96% is a long-run average, not a promise about tonight.

RTP is the share of every dollar wagered a game returns across millions of simulated spins. At 96%, the design returns 96 cents per dollar staked over the very long run.

A hundred spins can land far above it or far below it. Both are normal. Drag the sample size and watch the spread collapse toward the line.
