Reading the Odds

RTP, or Return to Player, is the theoretical percentage of every dollar wagered that a game pays back, calculated across millions of simulated spins. A 96% RTP means the game is designed to return 96 cents for every dollar staked, over the very long run.

96% is not what happens in any one session. That number is a long-run theoretical average, not a per-session guarantee. A hundred spins tonight can land far above it or far below it. Both are normal. The average only becomes visible at scale, the way a coin only settles near 50/50 after thousands of flips, not ten.

Compliance Posture

Built to Pass Inspection

Every Gambit Games title runs on the Stake Engine RGS. The math is not bolted on after launch, it is the foundation an auditor can actually check.

  • ✓

    Deterministic, Auditable RNG

    Every outcome runs through the math-sdk simulation pipeline, a fully reproducible chain from seed to payout with no black box in between.

  • ✓

    Replay-Mode by Default

    Every round ships with replay-mode support, so a regulator or auditor can step through any spin exactly as it was dealt.

  • ✓

    RTP Tolerance, Enforced in Testing

    Every bet mode is tested against a ±0.5% RTP tolerance band before it ever reaches a build, not adjusted after the fact.

  • ✓

    Mandatory Mute Control

    Sound control is wired into the engine on every title, not left for the player to dig through a menu to find.
